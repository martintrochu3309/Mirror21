STEP 05

  1. 01
    PAUSE

    Something happens, you feel it, and you react. The pause is the gap between the feeling and the reaction. The feeling is your signal to stop.

  2. 02
    CHOOSE

    In that gap, pick the response that moves you closer to where you want to be.

  3. 03
    RESPOND

    Do what you chose instead of the old reaction. Deciding it in advance makes it easier to do in the moment.

The gap between feeling and doing is where you get to choose.

EXAMPLE
THERE

I want to be able to wake up properly rested and ready for the day.

MY MOMENT

Tonight, when I’m about to say “just one more episode.”

THE FEELING

Tired, but not wanting the day to end.

PAUSE

One breath.

CHOOSE

What gets me closer to waking up rested? Switching it off.

IN THE BOX
When I’m about to start another episode, I will pause and switch the TV off.
YOUR TURN
