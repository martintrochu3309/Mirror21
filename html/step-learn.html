STEP 01

FROM HERE TO THERE.

Five moves. The same five Kevin just walked you through.

  1. 01
    PRESENT

    Stop and give your change your full attention.

  2. 02
    HERE

    Say where you are right now. Your real situation, not where you wish you were.

  3. 03
    THERE

    Say where you want to get to. Clear enough to picture.

  4. 04
    TEST

    Ask yourself if getting there will give you what you actually want.

  5. 05
    POINT & STEP

    Pick one thing you can do today to move that way.

You don’t need the whole route. You need a direction and a first step.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    PRESENT

    I’m here. Full attention.

  2. 02

    HERE

    Right now, I’m on my phone in bed most nights and wake up tired.

  3. 03

    THERE

    I want to be able to wake up properly rested and ready for the day.

  4. 04

    TEST

    Will getting there give me what I actually want? Yes.

  5. 05

    POINT & STEP

    Put my phone on charge across the room.

  6. MY PROMISE

    Today, I will put my phone on charge across the room.
