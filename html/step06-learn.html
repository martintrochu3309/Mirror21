STEP 06

  1. 01
    SPOT

    Your surroundings make some things easy and others hard. Find what’s making the old habit easy.

  2. 02
    SHAPE

    Add effort to the old habit and take effort away from the new one. The easier something is, the more likely you are to do it.

  3. 03
    USE

    A change only counts when it changes what you do. Set it up, then let it work for you in the moment.

Make the right thing easy and the wrong thing hard.

EXAMPLE
THERE

I want to be able to wake up properly rested and ready for the day.

WHAT MAKES THE OLD HABIT EASY

The TV remote lives on my bedside table.

MAKE THE OLD HABIT HARDER

The remote goes in a drawer in the living room.

MAKE THE NEW BEHAVIOUR EASIER

A book goes on my pillow.

IN THE BOX
Today I will put the TV remote in the living room and a book on my pillow.
