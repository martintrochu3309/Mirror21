STEP 11

  1. 01
    CHOOSE

    Pick someone you trust, who’ll take you seriously. You’re not asking for permission.

  2. 02
    TELL

    Share three things: where you’re going, what you’re doing differently, and your line. Keep it short.

  3. 03
    SET

    Name the day you’ll report back. You set it, so it doesn’t depend on anyone else.

  4. 04
    DO

    Have the conversation or send the message today. A promise someone else has heard is harder to quietly drop.

Ownership stays with you. Telling someone makes it harder to let go.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    CHOOSE

    Sam, a friend I trust.

  2. 02

    TELL

    “I’m working on waking up properly rested. I’m getting up at 6:30 every day, and swapping my phone for a book at 10pm. My line is ‘Be all there tomorrow.’”

  3. 03

    SET

    “I’ll message you Sunday and tell you how I got on.”

  4. 04

    DO

    I send Sam that message today.

  5. MY PROMISE

    Today, I will tell Sam what I’m changing, and that I will report back on Sunday.
