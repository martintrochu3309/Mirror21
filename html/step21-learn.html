STEP 21

  1. 01
    SCORE

    Your score shows how consistently you kept your promises. It isn’t a measure of you, or proof of change. You’ll see your score so far now, and your final score after tonight’s check.

  2. 02
    NEW HERE

    Put where you are today next to where you said you were in Step 1. Whatever it shows, write what’s true. That’s your evidence of change, in your own words.

  3. 03
    TWO CARDS

    One built with guidance in Step 19, one built on your own in Step 20. Look at what you chose, and what you actually did.

  4. 04
    WHAT’S NEXT

    Keep working on this change, run the steps again, or use the method on something new. Your score can help you decide. It can’t decide for you.

The score tells you how you followed through. Only you can say what changed.

    Step 20: A new change, a 20-minute walk at lunch. I did the first one.

    WHAT’S NEXT
