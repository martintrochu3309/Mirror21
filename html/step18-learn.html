STEP 18

  1. 01
    KEEP

    If something’s helping, keep it on purpose. What you keep by accident, you can lose by accident. If nothing is helping yet, say so.

  2. 02
    STOP

    Often it’s the part you owned in Step 17. If something needs stopping, name it. If nothing does, don’t invent a problem.

  3. 03
    START

    One small change, or a tool you’ve already learned but haven’t used enough. One is enough. Too many changes at once make it harder to follow through.

  4. 04
    BEGIN TODAY

    Take the first step on your start today. A plan that waits for a better week rarely begins.

Hindsight shows you what happened. Foresight decides what happens next.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    KEEP

    Using my minimum on hard mornings. It’s held every time I’ve used it.

  2. 02

    STOP

    Staying out past the time I said I’d leave.

  3. 03

    START

    Telling Sam my leaving time before a late night. I set up the report-back in Step 11 but haven’t used it for this.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Today I’ll message Sam my leaving time for Saturday’s dinner.
