STEP 15

  1. 01
    DROP THE STORY

    A miss is one day, not proof you can’t do it. One bad day only becomes a bad week if you let it.

  2. 02
    SET YOUR RESTART POINT

    Your next chance to do your move. Not Monday. Not next month.

  3. 03
    COME BACK SMALL

    Restart with your minimum. Don’t try to make up for the day you missed. Getting back in is the win.

  4. 04
    DECIDE IT NOW

    Set your restart rule before you need it, so a miss already has an answer.

Plan the comeback before you need it.

EXAMPLE
DROP THE STORY

The story I’d tell myself is “I’ve blown it this week, I’ll start again on Monday.”

SET YOUR RESTART POINT

My next chance, which is the next morning at 6:30.

COME BACK SMALL

My minimum. Feet on the floor at 6:30.

IN THE BOX
If I miss, I will restart the next morning with feet on the floor at 6:30.
