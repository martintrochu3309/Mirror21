STEP 13

  1. 01
    LOOK AHEAD

    Most hard days can be seen coming. Go through the next seven days and pick the one most likely to test your move.

  2. 02
    WALK THROUGH

    Picture that day from start to finish, and find the point where your move is most likely to slip.

  3. 03
    CHOOSE YOUR TOOL

    If the full move will be too hard, use your minimum. If the old habit will pull, use your swap and your line. Decide now, not on the day.

  4. 04
    GET READY

    Do one thing today that makes the plan easier: put it in your calendar, set things up in advance, or tell the person you’re reporting back to.

Decide on an easy day what you’ll do on a hard one.

EXAMPLE
LOOK AHEAD

Friday morning. I’m going to a friend’s birthday on Thursday night.

WALK THROUGH

I’ll be home after midnight. The slip point is Friday at 6:30, when the alarm goes.

CHOOSE YOUR TOOL

My minimum. Feet on the floor at 6:30, even if I’m slow after that.

MY PROMISE
On Friday, I will do at least my minimum: get my feet on the floor at 6:30, even if I’m slow after that. Today, I will put “Friday 6:30, feet on the floor” in my calendar and set Friday’s alarm now.
