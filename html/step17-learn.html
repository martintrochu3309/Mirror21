STEP 17

  1. 01
    AS IT HAPPENED

    Pick one moment, even if no clear pattern stood out. Say what happened, reasons and all. Check whether your Step 15 story showed up.

  2. 02
    SEPARATE

    What happened around you, and what you did. Both can be true. Reasons explain the circumstances. They don’t cancel your part.

  3. 03
    OWN

    Accept what you did, without blaming yourself or anyone else. That part is yours, and that’s what lets you do something about it.

  4. 04
    PUT IT TO WORK

    If something slipped, do one thing differently today. If things held, do one thing today that helps them hold again.

You can’t own what happened to you. You can own what you did.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    AS IT HAPPENED

    The friend’s birthday last Thursday. I didn’t do my full move on Friday. My Step 15 story showed up too: “I’ve blown it this week.”

  2. 02

    SEPARATE

    Around me: the night ran late. What I did: I stayed for another hour after saying I’d leave at 11.

  3. 03

    OWN

    That hour was mine. The birthday didn’t keep me there.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Today I’ll set a 10:45 “time to go” alarm for Saturday’s dinner.
