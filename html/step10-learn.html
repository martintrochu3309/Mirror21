STEP 10

  1. 01
    DIG

    Your first answer is usually the surface one. Ask why again until you reach something you really care about.

  2. 02
    YOURS

    A reason that’s genuinely yours holds when it’s hard. A reason borrowed from someone else doesn’t.

  3. 03
    ONE LINE

    Turn your reason into a line short enough to say in a single breath, in your own words.

  4. 04
    USE

    When the old habit pulls, pause, say your line, then make your swap. The line carries you from the pause to the swap.

You don’t need more motivation. You need your reason ready.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    DIG

    “I want more energy.” What’s it for? “To get through the day properly.” Why does that matter? “Because I’m tired of only being half there.”

  2. 02

    YOURS

    Yes. That’s mine.

  3. 03

    ONE LINE

    “Be all there tomorrow.”

  4. 04

    USE

    At 10pm on the sofa, I’ll pause, say “Be all there tomorrow”, and pick up my book.

  5. MY PROMISE

    Today, when I’m on the sofa at about 10pm and the day’s done, I will pause, say “Be all there tomorrow”, and pick up my book. If the moment doesn’t come, I will run through it once in my head.
