STEP 19

  1. 01
    SEE IT

    Awareness. Look at where you want to go and what’s actually happening now. Let the facts lead, not the feeling.

  2. 02
    OWN IT

    Acceptance. Name your part, whether it’s helping or holding things back. No blame, either way.

  3. 03
    CHANGE IT

    Action. Choose your move (usually the one you already have), then the one tool that fits it: your anchor, your swap, your minimum or your restart rule.

  4. 04
    USE IT TODAY

    Put all three on your card, then take one real action from it today.

Tools are what you know. A method is how you use them.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    SEE IT

    I want to wake up properly rested. Weekday mornings are holding. Mornings after late nights still slip.

  2. 02

    OWN IT

    Getting up on weekdays is mine, and it’s working. Staying out past my leaving time is mine too.

  3. 03

    CHANGE IT

    My move stays the same: up at 6:30. Today’s tool is my anchor.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Tonight, after I brush my teeth, I’ll put my phone on charge in the kitchen.
