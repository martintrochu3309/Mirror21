STEP 04

  1. 01
    SPOT

    Name what could get in the way of your move.

  2. 02
    SORT

    Control is what you can choose or do yourself. Influence is what you can affect but can’t decide. Out of your hands is everything else, so you park it.

  3. 03
    CONTROL

    You only ever act on what’s in your control. That’s also how you reach what you can influence.

Energy spent on what you can’t change is energy taken from what you can.

EXAMPLE
MOVE

Get up at the same time every day.

IN THE WAY
  • I hit snooze.
  • My partner watches TV in bed.
  • Some nights I just sleep badly.
SORT
  • CONTROLHitting snooze.
  • INFLUENCEMy partner’s TV. I can ask, but I can’t decide.
  • OUT OF MY HANDSThe odd bad night. Park it.
IN THE BOX
Today I will put my alarm across the room so I have to get up to turn it off.
YOUR TURN
