STEP 14

  1. 01
    EVIDENCE

    Look at your checks, not your feelings. Have you been doing the full move most days, or leaning on your minimum?

  2. 02
    DECIDE

    If it’s been the full move, raise the bar. If it’s been the minimum, make it hold. Both are progress.

  3. 03
    STEP UP

    Raising the bar means one small increase: a bit earlier, a bit longer, one more time a week. Start it today. Making it hold means doing the full move today, not the minimum.

  4. 04
    PROTECT

    Keep your minimum, your anchor and your swap. A higher bar needs a solid base.

Let the evidence decide, not your mood.

  2. 02

    DECIDE

  3. 03

    STEP UP

  4. 04

    PROTECT

    My minimum, my anchor and my swap stay exactly as they are.

  5. MY PROMISE

    Today, I will start my new bar: up and out of bed at 6:30, then dressed and ready to start my morning within ten minutes.
