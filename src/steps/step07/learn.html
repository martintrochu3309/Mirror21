STEP 07

  1. 01
    ANCHOR

    Something you already do every day without thinking. Pick one that happens at the time your action needs to happen. A night-time action needs a night-time anchor.

  2. 02
    ATTACH

    Say it as one line: after I [anchor], I’ll [action]. If you’ve got actions that belong together, line them up after the same anchor.

  3. 03
    FOLLOW THROUGH

    Do it straight after the anchor, every time. The more you repeat the link, the less you have to think about it.

Don’t rely on remembering. Rely on what you already do.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    ANCHOR

    Brushing my teeth at night.

  2. 02

    ATTACH

    After I brush my teeth, I’ll put my phone on charge in the kitchen, then read my book in bed.

  3. 03

    FOLLOW THROUGH

    Every night, straight after brushing my teeth. No deciding.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Today, after I brush my teeth at night, I will put my phone on charge in the kitchen, then read my book in bed.
