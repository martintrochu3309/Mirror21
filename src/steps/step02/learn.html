STEP 02

THERE PROOF ONCE.

Three moves. The same three Kevin just walked you through.

  1. 01
    THERE

    Your destination from Step 1. Everything you do in this step points at it.

    YOURS FROM STEP 01

  2. 02
    PROOF

    An action is something you could prove you did. If you couldn’t prove it, it’s still an intention.

  3. 03
    ONCE

    Doing it once turns an intention into experience. After that, you know what doing looks like.

A destination tells you where you’re going. An action tells you what to do today.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    THERE

    I want to be able to wake up properly rested and ready for the day.

  2. 02

    PROOF

    Not “be more disciplined at bedtime”. That’s an intention. “Phone outside the bedroom by 10pm”: I either did it or I didn’t.

  3. 03

    ONCE

    Just tonight, once. Then I’ll know what doing it looks like.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Today, I will put my phone outside the bedroom by 10pm.
