STEP 12

  1. 01
    MOVE

    Start with the move you chose in Step 3. Your minimum is a smaller version of that move, not something new.

  2. 02
    SHRINK

    Keep the same action and the same target. Cut how much of it you do.

  3. 03
    TEST

    Could you do it on your worst day? Does it still count as the same move? It has to pass both.

  4. 04
    USE

    Do the full version when you can, and the minimum when you can’t. Either one keeps the move alive.

The minimum isn’t the goal. It’s what keeps you going on the days you’d otherwise stop.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    MOVE

    Get up at 6:30 every day, weekends included.

  2. 02

    SHRINK

    Not “on bad days, get up at 7:30”. That moves the target. “Feet on the floor at 6:30, even if I’m slow after that”: same time, less effort.

  3. 03

    TEST

    Could I do it on my worst day? Yes. Is it still the same move? Yes.

  4. 04

    USE

    Up at 6:30 when I can. Feet on the floor at 6:30 when I can’t.

  5. MY PROMISE

    Today, I will do my move, or at least get my feet on the floor at 6:30, even if I’m slow after that.
