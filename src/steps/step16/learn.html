STEP 16

  1. 01
    RECORD

    Your YES, PARTLY and NO checks are a record of what you did, not a verdict on who you are.

  2. 02
    REPEATS

    Your checks show how you followed through on each step. They don’t tell you how often you’ve done your move. For that, think back to the times your move was due. What kept happening? Where did you follow through, and where did it slip? If nothing clear stands out yet, that’s a finding too.

  3. 03
    PROMISES

    Check the commitments you made. Did you report back on the day you set? If your hard day has passed, did the plan hold?

  4. 04
    NAME IT

    One thing that’s working, and one thing that isn’t. Just the facts. Then send them to your person, in as much detail as you choose.

One day is an event. A pattern is information.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    RECORD

    Most of my step checks are YES. Two are NO.

  2. 02

    REPEATS

    Looking back at my mornings, getting up at 6:30 is harder after late nights.

  3. 03

    PROMISES

    I reported back to Sam on Sunday. On my planned hard day, I used my minimum.

  4. 04

    NAME IT

    Working: using my minimum when I need it. Not working yet: doing my full move after late nights.

  5. MY PROMISE

    Today I’ll message Sam: “My minimum held on Friday, but getting up properly at 6:30 after late nights still needs work.”
