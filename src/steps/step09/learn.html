STEP 09

  1. 01
    MOMENT

    Your old habit shows up at a predictable time and place. That’s where the change has to happen.

  2. 02
    PAUSE

    The pause from Step 5 creates the gap. Without it, the old habit runs before you’ve decided anything.

  3. 03
    SWAP

    Replace the old habit with something that gives you what it gave you, and still moves you towards where you want to be. If it doesn’t, you’ll keep getting pulled back.

Don’t just stop the old habit. Give it a better replacement.

EXAMPLE

  1. 01

    MOMENT

    About 10pm on the sofa, when I’d normally scroll for 45 minutes to switch off.

  2. 02

    PAUSE

    I reach for my phone, and stop. One breath.

  3. 03

    SWAP

    Pick up my book. Does it still switch me off? Yes. Does it move me towards waking up rested? Yes.

  4. MY PROMISE

    Today, when I’m on the sofa at about 10pm and the day’s done, I will pause and pick up my book. If the moment doesn’t come, I will run through it once in my head.
